### About Me

I'm a Medical Bioinformatics master's student at Sri Ramachandra Institute of Higher Education and Research, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. My background is a bit unconventional — I started out as a licensed Physician Assistant, working hands-on in clinics and hair transplant surgical care, before moving into computational biology.

Right now I work primarily with **Python, R, C, SQL/DBMS**, and **PyMOL**, and I'm deep in cancer genomics — specifically tertiary EGFR resistance mutations in lung cancer, using data from **cBioPortal** and **COSMIC**. I'm also building up my skills in computational structural biology.

My motto: *Clinical intuition, computational execution.* Or as I like to put it — I went from setting up IV lines to setting up R pipelines.

---

### 🧬 Currently Working On

Scoping a master's thesis on **lesser-studied tertiary EGFR resistance mutations in lung cancer** (L792H, G796R, G796D, L718Q) — moving beyond the well-characterized C797S mutation. Working with a guide whose expertise spans transcriptomics, proteomics, ab initio modeling, and structural docking. Data sourced via cBioPortal and COSMIC.
